Three little pigs decided to build homes at the edge of a forest. The first wanted to play and quickly made a house of straw. The second used wood and finished that afternoon. The third chose bricks, laid a strong foundation, and worked for several days.

One morning a hungry wolf appeared. He knocked at the first house, and the frightened pig shut the door. The wolf blew hard, and the straw flew away. The little pig ran to his brother’s wooden house.

The wolf followed and blew again. The boards began to shake, so the two brothers ran together to the brick house. The third pig welcomed them in, gave them water, and secured the door. When the wolf blew, the bricks did not move.

The wolf looked for another way in, but the three pigs stayed together and called their neighbors. Seeing everyone gathered, the wolf went back into the forest. That night, the brothers rested in a safe home.

The next day, nobody laughed at the fallen houses. They cleared the ground and built two new homes with strong foundations. The straw-house brother organized the supplies, the wood-house brother made doors, and the brick-house brother showed them how to lay bricks. Working together made all three houses better.
