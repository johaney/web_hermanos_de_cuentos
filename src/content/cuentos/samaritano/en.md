Jesus once told a story about a traveler on a long road. On the way, he was hurt and sat by the roadside, waiting for help.

Some people passed by in a hurry. They saw him but kept walking. Then a man from Samaria arrived. Although he did not know the traveler, he came over and asked how he was.

The Samaritan cared for his wounds, helped him onto his donkey, and found a safe place for him to rest. Before leaving, he made sure the traveler would be looked after.

At the inn, the traveler had water and a place to rest. The Samaritan spoke to the innkeeper: “Please look after him until he feels better.” He left what was needed to help with his stay.

When Jesus finished the story, he asked who had truly been a good neighbor. The people listening thought of the man who had stopped. He had not waited to learn the traveler’s name before helping him.

With this story, Jesus taught that being a good neighbor means caring for someone who needs help, even when they are a stranger.
