A lion was sleeping under a tree when a little mouse ran across his mane. The lion woke and caught him under his paw.

“Let me go,” said the mouse. “Perhaps one day I can help you.” The lion laughed but lifted his paw and let him leave.

Days later, a net trapped the lion. He roared so loudly that the mouse heard him from far away. The mouse ran over and chewed through the ropes, one by one, until the lion was free.

Before leaving, the mouse sat beside the lion to catch his breath. The lion thanked him in a voice much softer than his roar. “I thought only big animals could help me,” he admitted.

The mouse smiled and reminded him that small teeth had been exactly what the net needed. From that day on, the lion listened more carefully to the forest animals and never judged a friend by their size again.

“You were right,” said the lion. “Help can come from someone we least expect.” And they went on their way as friends.
