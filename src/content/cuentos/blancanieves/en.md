Snow White lived in a castle with a queen who wanted everyone to admire her. One day, the queen grew angry when she thought the young girl was more loved than she was. Snow White went into the forest to find somewhere peaceful and safe.

After walking among the pines, she found a cottage with seven tiny chairs. Its owners were seven friends who worked in the mountains. When they returned, they listened to her story and invited her to stay. Snow White cooked with them, learned their songs, and felt welcome.

The queen found out where she was and visited the cottage disguised as a seller. She offered Snow White a shiny apple. Snow White took a bite and fell into a deep sleep. When her friends returned, they found her and asked their neighbors for help.

A young traveler arrived with a healer from the village. As they carefully examined Snow White, a small piece of apple caught in her throat came loose. Snow White woke, drank some water, and saw her friends gathered around her.

The queen could no longer fool anyone in the forest. Snow White chose to live near the people who had cared for her and kept visiting the cottage with seven chairs. She had learned that friendship and trust could make a home.
