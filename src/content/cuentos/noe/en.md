According to the Bible story, God asked Noah to build a large ark. Noah made room for his family and for animals of many kinds.

Heavy rain came. Inside the ark, everyone waited together and cared for one another. Many days passed before the rain stopped and dry land appeared.

When they could go outside, a colorful rainbow appeared in the sky. For Noah and his family, it was a sign of hope and a new beginning.

While they waited, everyone had a task. Noah checked the ark, his family shared the food, and they watched over the animals to keep them calm. Some days were noisy and some were quiet, but they kept caring for one another.

When the water began to fall, Noah sent out a bird to look for signs of land. The waiting ended little by little. Outside, they felt the ground under their feet and saw sunlight fill the landscape again.

Whenever we see a rainbow, we can remember that story and think about how we care for the world we share.
