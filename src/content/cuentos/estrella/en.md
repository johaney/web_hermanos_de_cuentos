High in the sky lived Lia, a little star who guarded her light carefully. Each night she shone only a little, afraid of running out of brightness.

One evening, a dark cloud covered the path of a bird looking for his nest. “Could you lend me a little light?” he asked. Lia hesitated, then lit one of her points to show him the way.

The bird found his tree and sang with joy. Other stars heard him and lit up the sky too. Lia was surprised: her light was still there, warm and bright.

The next day, Lia saw a little boat far from shore. Clouds hid the moon, and the people aboard were looking for the lighthouse. Lia called her star friends, and together they drew a path of light across the water.

When the boat reached the harbor, nobody knew who had guided it. Lia did not need them to know. She looked at her friends and understood that sharing could bring them together too.

From then on, whenever someone needed help, Lia shared her glow. And the more she shared, the lovelier the sky became.
