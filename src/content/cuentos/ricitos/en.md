One morning, three bears made porridge for breakfast. It was too hot, so they went for a walk in the woods. Soon Goldilocks came upon their cottage and knocked. Nobody answered.

Curious, Goldilocks went inside without permission. She found three bowls on the table. The large bowl was too hot, the medium bowl too cold, and the small bowl was just right, so she ate it. Then she tried the chairs. The large one was too hard, the medium one too soft, and the small one felt perfect—but it broke.

Upstairs, the large bed felt too firm and the medium one too soft. She fell asleep in the small bed. When the bears returned, they saw the bowls, the broken chair, and finally Goldilocks sleeping.

Goldilocks woke with a start. Seeing the bears, she realized she had entered someone else’s home and used things that were not hers. She apologized for the porridge and the chair. The bears listened and asked her to wait for someone to open the door next time.

Goldilocks helped mend the chair with a little glue and went home. The next day she left a thank-you note at their door. She had learned that curiosity can lead us to wonderful discoveries when we respect the people who care for them.
