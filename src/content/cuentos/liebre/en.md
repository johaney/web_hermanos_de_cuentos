The hare ran so fast that she teased the tortoise. One day the tortoise suggested a race. The forest animals gathered to watch them start.

The hare raced ahead. Far in the lead, she decided to rest under a tree. The tortoise kept walking, step by step, without stopping.

When the hare woke, she saw the tortoise close to the finish line. She ran as fast as she could, but the tortoise arrived first.

At first, the hare felt embarrassed. The tortoise came over and offered her some water. “You ran very fast,” she said, “but I knew I could get there if I kept moving.”

The animals cheered for both runners. The following week they walked through the woods together: the hare learned to enjoy slow steps, and the tortoise discovered new paths with her friend.

The hare learned to respect others. The tortoise celebrated her effort: she had not been fast, but she had kept going.
